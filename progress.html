Hydration Tips

💧Drink water before, during, and after exercise to stay hydrated and perform better.
🌅Start your morning with a glass of water to kickstart your metabolism.
🏃During intense workouts, aim for 150–250ml of water every 15–20 minutes.
🌡️In hot weather or during heavy exercise, increase your intake by 1–2 extra glasses.
🍋Add lemon or mint to your water if plain water feels boring — it helps you drink more.
⚠️Signs of dehydration: dark urine, headache, fatigue, dizziness. Drink up!
