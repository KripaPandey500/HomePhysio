About HomePhysio

Making physiotherapy accessible, guided and trackable for everyone — right from the comfort of home.

Our Mission

HomePhysio was built as an internship project to help physiotherapy patients follow their prescribed exercise routines at home — without needing printed papers, expensive apps, or visiting a clinic.

We believe recovery should be simple, guided and consistent. HomePhysio gives every patient a personal exercise plan, clear step-by-step instructions, countdown timers for each exercise, and automatic progress tracking — all stored in a real MongoDB database.

What HomePhysio Offers

Everything patients need in one place

14+ Exercises

A complete library covering neck, shoulder, back, knee, ankle, leg and hand — each with real images and detailed step-by-step instructions.

Custom Routines

Patients build personalized routines by selecting exercises, setting repetitions and cycles, saved directly to MongoDB.

Guided Workout Timer

A built-in countdown timer guides patients through each set, with automatic rest periods and clear visual indicators.

Progress Tracking

Every completed workout is auto-saved. View history, day streak, total workouts and a 7-week activity calendar heatmap.

Admin Dashboard

Admins can add, edit and delete exercises, view all registered patients and monitor every workout log from one dashboard.

Works Everywhere

Fully responsive — works on desktop, laptop, tablet and mobile. No app installation needed.
