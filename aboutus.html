About HomePhysio

Making physiotherapy accessible, guided and trackable for everyone — right from the comfort of home.

Our Mission

HomePhysio was built as an internship project to help physiotherapy patients follow their prescribed exercise routines at home — without needing printed papers, expensive apps, or visiting a clinic.

We believe recovery should be simple, guided and consistent. HomePhysio gives every patient a personal exercise plan, clear step-by-step instructions, countdown timers for each exercise, and automatic progress tracking — all stored in a real MongoDB database.

What HomePhysio Offers

Everything patients need in one place

14+ Exercises

A complete library of physiotherapy exercises covering neck, shoulder, back, knee, ankle, leg and hand — each with real images and detailed instructions.

Custom Routines

Patients can build their own personalized routines by selecting exercises, setting repetitions and cycles, and saving them to the MongoDB database.

Guided Workout Timer

A built-in countdown timer guides patients through each set, with automatic rest periods between sets and clear visual indicators.

Progress Tracking

Every completed workout is automatically saved. Patients can view their history, day streak, total workouts and an activity calendar.

Admin Dashboard

Administrators can add, edit and delete exercises, view all registered patients, and monitor workout logs from a dedicated dashboard.

Works Everywhere

Fully responsive design works on desktop, laptop, tablet and mobile — patients can use it on any device without installing anything.
